Questions, answered plainly

The things a skeptic asks first

Why won't ChatGPT just replace this?
A chat answers a question and forgets you. CDIS maintains a persistent, verified, longitudinal model of who you are and learns from real outcomes. The language model is one replaceable component — the moat is the Twin, the evidence graph, and the outcome loop.
Do you show a "chance of getting hired"?
No — and it's enforced at the API contract level, not just the UI. You get an Alignment Score (0–100) with named limiting factors, supporting evidence, and estimated improvement. Fake-precise probabilities are exactly what we refuse to ship.
What happens when the AI is wrong — or down?
Numbers never come from a model, so it can't fabricate them. If the model is slow or offline, a deterministic rule engine takes over and the output is honestly labeled as a fallback. Every recommendation is replayable, so you can always inspect exactly how it was produced.
Is my data actually mine?
Yes. Full export and hard delete, encryption in transit and at rest, no PII in logs or URLs. Outcome data (offers, rejections) is recorded only with explicit consent and is never used across users without separate consent.
Why do certificates barely move my score?
Because a certificate proves you attended, not that you can build. It's capped at Medium on its own by design. Add a repo, a deployment, or a shipped project and the same skill climbs — because now there's something to verify.
Do I need to set anything up to try it?
No. Create an account, paste a resume, and you're in the loop in under a minute. No API keys, no configuration — the deterministic engine runs with zero external dependencies.
